## Question

List the complete playable roster of each MVS release (hidden, unlockable and bosses included) and classify each fighter as a Shared Character or an Exclusive Character in each direction (KOF 2003 as Base Game, and SVC Chaos as Base Game).

_PT-BR: listar o elenco jogável completo de cada versão MVS (ocultos, liberáveis e chefes) e classificar cada um como personagem compartilhado ou exclusivo em cada direção (KOF 2003 como jogo base e SVC Chaos como jogo base)._

## Notes

- Roster count rule (map Notes): every playable fighter in some way, bosses with lower weight.
- Only the MVS releases count here; console-only additions are noted separately, not counted.

_PT-BR: regra de contagem do mapa: todos os jogáveis de alguma forma, chefes com peso menor. Só as versões MVS contam; acréscimos exclusivos de console ficam anotados à parte._

## Resolution

**Counting rule approved by the owner.** Count the official MVS selection, including fighters accessible by built-in codes. Give playable bosses and mid-bosses weight 0.5, including Chizuru by her arcade role, and all other fighters weight 1. Count Orochi Iori as an Exclusive Character because he has his own slot and moveset. The six Shared Characters are Kyo Kusanagi, Iori Yagami, Terry Bogard, Ryo Sakazaki, Kim Kaphwan and Mai Shiranui. SVC's Princess Athena and KOF 2003's Athena Asamiya are distinct fighters.

| Base Game | Base roster (raw / weighted) | Exclusive Characters in the Donor Game (raw / weighted) |
|---|---:|---:|
| KOF 2003 | 34 / 33 | 26 / 22 from SVC (18 regular + 8 bosses or mid-bosses) |
| SVC Chaos | 32 / 28 | 28 / 27 from KOF 2003 (26 regular + 2 bosses or mid-bosses) |

The research branch has the full rosters and classifications. Its broader SVC count of 36 adds Shin Akuma and Serious Mr. Karate via AES and Princess Athena and Red Arremer via cheat or hack. Adelheid and Mukai are outside the official KOF 2003 MVS selection. The exact form of the Chizuru unlocked by code and possible cheat access to Adelheid and Mukai remain unverified. Those checks do not change the approved counting rule.
